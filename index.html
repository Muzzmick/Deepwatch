🎣 Rigs & Baits by Species
Bream & Whiting
Running sinker rig, #4-6 hook, live yabby, worm, pilchard. Fish close to structure on run-out tide.
Flathead
Soft plastics on 1/8oz jighead, trolling hardbodies over sand. Low tide edges and channel drop-offs.
Mulloway
Large live bait (mullet/herring), fish the bridge pylons after dark on the run-in tide near new/full moon.
Snapper (offshore)
Flasher rig, whole pilchard or squid. Berley trail. Fish at anchor over reef at 30-40m.
Tuna & Game
Trolling lures 6-8kn, skirted lures or stick baits. Find the warm water eddy line for tuna and billfish.
Prawning
Dip net or scoop net, torch to spot eyes, calm nights on new/full moon Nov-Apr in shallow weedy areas.
⚠️ Bar Crossing Safety
🟢 Safe: Swell under 1.0m, wind under 15kn
🟡 Caution: Swell 1.0–1.5m, or wind 15–20kn
🔴 Dangerous: Swell over 1.5m or wind over 20kn
Always check BOM marine forecast before crossing. Conditions can change rapidly. When in doubt, don't go out.
