⚠️ Closed Seasons & Rules
🚫 Gippsland Lakes Marine National Park
No fishing in designated sanctuary zones. Check VFA maps before heading out.
📅 Dusky Flathead Slot
30–55cm only. All fish outside this range must be released immediately.
🎣 Recreational Fishing Licence
Required for all anglers 18+. Get yours at vfa.vic.gov.au or Service Victoria.
📞 Report Illegal Fishing
VFA Hotline: 13 FISH (13 3474) — anonymous reporting available.
🎣 Best Rigs & Baits
Snapper: Whole pilchard or squid, running sinker, 4/0–6/0 circle hook
Bream: Prawns, nippers, soft plastics. Light 1–2kg line, size 4–8 hook
Flathead: Soft plastics 3–4", minnow lures. Slow retrieve along bottom
Whiting: Nippers, beach worms. Running sinker, size 4–6 long shank
Mulloway: Live mullet or herring. Fish after dark near channel edges
Salmon: Metal lures, poppers. Cast into surf gutters on beach
Trevally: Small metal slugs, live bait. Fish drop-offs and channel edges
